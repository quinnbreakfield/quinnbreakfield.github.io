About Quinn

A love for creating

I have always had a passion for creating things. There is a unique satisfaction in fixing a pesky bug, coding a video game event, and designing a website. At the heart of these issues is a large degree of tedium. But they are incredibly rewarding.

Not because the tasks are monumental on their own, but because they contribute to a larger goal and vision. And through my experience with projects in different mediums, I continue to grow. I mature both in that skill and as a designer.

Simplicity as an ideal

The last thing anyone wants to feel is overwhelmed. A good design is effective. A great design is simultaneously effective and simple. Form follows function is the motto I stand by, and it's evident in all my work.

Design is an art, yes, but it is also a thought process. Considering the different ways a product may be used is essential to creating something intuitive and universal. Keeping the thoughts and motives of the user in mind drives user-centered design.

Ready to adapt

It's unknown how the technology of the future will shape daily life. But I'm prepared to adapt accordingly. AI has caused a major panic for many developers, rightfully so. There's no doubt it will transform the landscape of our economy and society.

Currently, I approach AI cautiously, respecting its uses, but heeding the concerns many critics present. To me, AI should be a tool, not a crutch. I use it to critique my designs, not make them.

For instance, I initially sketched the structure of this website, and all the text was drafted by me. A person. One capable of the occasional spelling misatkemistake. Though this website was largely coded by hand, AI did help with bug fixes and inform decisions along the way.
